Welcome to Sports Games: The Ultimate Daily Trivia Portal

Sports Games is the ultimate online platform for sports fans looking to challenge their athletic knowledge daily. We host an array of interactive daily puzzles, grid games, and player guessing quizzes covering Cricket, Football/Soccer, Formula 1, and NBA Basketball. Our entire website is fully autonomous, meaning a brand new puzzle for every game mode generates at midnight every 24 hours without requiring any manual updates or backend servers.

How it works: choose your sport and game mode. Test your skills in our 3x3 Daily Grid games, where you match players who satisfy two intersecting criteria. Or play our Player Guesser, where you get hot/cold hints based on details like team, position, nationality, and stats to deduce a mystery athlete. Finally, try the Career Path trivia game, which traces a player's chronological club/constructor trade timeline, challenging you to guess the player in 6 attempts.

Our tools compile data from major open-source datasets (like StatsBomb, Ergast F1, and Cricsheet) to support robust, clean autocomplete queries and instant player validations locally in your browser memory. Best of all, because the site runs 100% client-side, your statistics, streaks, and played grids are permanently saved in your browser storage with $0 hosting cost. Play daily, improve your stats, and share your score grids with your friends today!
